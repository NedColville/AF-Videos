Figure 1

Figure 1a

Example of a GU during a large geomagnetic storm, characterised by its dusk sector equatorward undulations. In this video we see how the wave and its crests propagate sunward. Event identified in a 2010 Henderson paper.

Figure 1b

Example of an omega band, characterised by its poleward, dawn sector undulations. Similarly to the GU, we see sunward, but this time eastward propagation. Event identified by our model .

Figure 6

Figure 6a

An omega band event identified by our model. We see a a large train of omega bands spanning over 6 MLT.

Figure 6b

Omega band event identified by our model. This event morphologically is interesting, with tall, thin torch/streamer like structures and fast sunward flows after the event.

Figure 6c

This GU event, initially published in a 2007 paper, despite being unseen, was recovered by our model. Morphologically, this event certainly has smaller amplitudes than figure 1a, but still is recovered, showing the strong generalisation of the model. Again, we see sunward propagation.

Figure 6d

A rare dawnside, possibly GU event recovered by our model. Although more difficult to see than other examples, this event poses interesting questions as to the drivers of auroral forms given the MLT at which it occurs.
